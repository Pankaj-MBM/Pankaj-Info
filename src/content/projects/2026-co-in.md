## Overview

**2026.co.in** is a complete QR ordering system for cafés and restaurants. Guests scan the QR code on their table or room and place the order themselves, while the business runs menus, staff and orders for every branch from one place. It is live in production.

## What the platform covers

- **QR scan & order** — QR codes for tables, rooms and other spots, so guests can order without waiting for staff.
- **Multi-branch management** — menus, staff and orders across all of a business’s cafés in one admin panel.
- **Platform administration** — onboarding cafés with a go-live check, and managing active and suspended cafés.
- **Subscriptions & billing** — plans, subscriptions, invoices, payments, coupons, overrides and add-ons.
