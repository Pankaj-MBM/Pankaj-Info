## Overview

An online catalog for a precision engineering-parts business. Buyers browse categories, open a product and **rotate it in 3D** before adding it to the cart.

## Highlights

- **360° viewer** — loads 3D models (GLTF) with React Three Fiber, with orbit controls, zoom and a fallback model if a file fails to load.
- **Catalog** — categories, product grid, carousel and product detail pages.
- **Cart & checkout** — shared cart state, a cart page and a checkout flow.
- **Themes** — light and dark modes.

## Build

Built with an **AI-assisted workflow** (Lovable) in **React + TypeScript** with **Tailwind CSS** and **shadcn/ui**; 3D with **three.js** through **React Three Fiber** and **Drei**.
